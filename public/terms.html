Escrow & M-Pesa Payment Policy

💡
Plain language: When you book, your M-Pesa payment is held safely by the platform. The artisan only receives it after you confirm the job is done — this protects both of you.

Payment at Booking

When a Client confirms a Booking, the total amount due (job price plus the client booking fee) must be paid via M-Pesa STK Push through the Platform. Payments by card (Visa/Mastercard) are also accepted where available. The Booking is not confirmed until payment is successfully received by the Platform's escrow account.

Escrow Holding Period

Funds paid by the Client are held in escrow by the Company and are not released to the Artisan until one of the following occurs:

  • The Client confirms through the Platform that the Service has been completed satisfactorily; or
  • Forty-eight (48) hours have passed since the Artisan marked the job as complete, and the Client has not raised a Dispute or confirmed non-completion; or
  • A Dispute is resolved in favour of the Artisan by the Company's dispute resolution process.

Artisan Payout

Payouts are made to the Artisan's registered M-Pesa number after the release conditions above are met. Commission is deducted automatically before payout. The Artisan's in-app wallet reflects the status of all pending and completed payouts.

Failed or Incomplete Payments

If an M-Pesa payment fails or is reversed for any reason, the Booking will not be confirmed. The Company is not responsible for delays or failures in M-Pesa transactions caused by Safaricom or the Artisan's mobile network.

🛡️
Your payment is protected: Funds are never released to an Artisan before you have had the opportunity to confirm satisfactory completion or raise a dispute. This applies to all in-app payments.

Disputes & Resolution

💡
Plain language: If there's a problem with a job, raise it through the app within 48 hours. We will mediate. If we cannot resolve it, you can go to a Kenyan court — but please try us first.

Raising a Dispute

A Client may raise a Dispute within forty-eight (48) hours of the Artisan marking a job as complete. Disputes raised after this window may not be eligible for escrow intervention. To raise a Dispute, the Client must use the in-app dispute function, provide a description of the issue, and where possible, submit supporting evidence such as photographs or chat records.

Dispute Resolution Process

On receipt of a Dispute, the Company will:

  1. Freeze the escrow payment and notify both the Client and Artisan within twenty-four (24) hours;
  2. Request responses and evidence from both parties within three (3) business days;
  3. Review the evidence and make a determination within five (5) business days of receiving all responses; and
  4. Communicate the outcome to both parties and release or refund the escrowed funds accordingly.

Company's Decision

The Company's dispute determination is final and binding for the purposes of releasing escrow funds. The Company acts as a neutral mediator and does not guarantee any particular outcome. The Company's decision on escrow does not affect either party's right to pursue legal remedies through the Kenyan courts.

Good Faith Requirement

Both parties are required to engage in the dispute process in good faith. Abuse of the dispute system — including raising false disputes, withholding payment without legitimate reason, or providing fabricated evidence — may result in account suspension.

ℹ️
The Company is not a judicial body. For disputes involving significant financial amounts or potential criminal conduct, Users are encouraged to seek independent legal advice and, where appropriate, involve the relevant Kenyan authorities including the Directorate of Criminal Investigations (DCI).
